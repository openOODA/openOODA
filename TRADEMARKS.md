## Permitted & Restricted Uses

- **Allowed without permission:**
  - Truthful descriptive statements (e.g., *"Built with openOODA"*, *"Compiled with `ooda-compiler`"*, *"Forked from openOODA `vX.Y.Z`"*).
  - Unmodified redistribution of official signed openOODA releases matching published `SHA256SUMS` and `ooda-sign` signatures.
- **Not allowed without written permission:**
  - Distributing modified source trees, compilers, kernels, OS images, or packages (especially forks that disable capability checks, taint tracking, sandbox confinement, or telemetry protections) under the names **"openOODA"**, **"Official openOODA"**, or with confusingly similar branding implying official endorsement.
